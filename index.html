Before work begins

Make written quotes line up before you choose.

Is your home an older one with past alterations? If so, wiring of different ages and methods may sit behind the same walls, and records of who changed what can be partial or missing. A newer house may lack paperwork for later changes.

Give every contractor the same written description, photographs of visible labels and any paperwork you hold, so each quote covers the same scope. Then compare inclusions, exclusions, notification and wall or ceiling restoration line by line.

Why do written quotes for identical work read so differently?

Have each contractor restate the scope in the same order: what is replaced, what is reused, what is excluded and who files the ESA notification. A quote that is silent on a point has not agreed to it.

What if my home has no record of earlier electrical changes?

Say so in the request and let each contractor explain how they would deal with unknown wiring. Compare how the quotes treat surprises found behind finished walls, since an allowance or a stated exclusion changes what you may pay later.

Does a shorter written quote mean less work is covered?

Not necessarily, but a short quote leaves more to assumption. Check whether it names the equipment, the notification, the restoration of opened surfaces and the items left out, and ask the contractor to add whatever is missing.

Who can say which wiring in an older house was altered earlier?

Previous owners, the seller's disclosure, old inspection reports and ESA paperwork may help. A contractor can describe what is visible, but should state in writing which conclusions rest on assumption rather than on records.

Will anyone visit my house just because I sent details here?

No. A provider or Licensed Electrical Contractor, once you choose one, arranges visits and timing directly, settles scope and price, and separately confirms its identity and licence. Check that licence through ESA before agreeing. This website, a collection of guides, reviews each enquiry received but arranges no visits or prices and carries out no electrical work.
